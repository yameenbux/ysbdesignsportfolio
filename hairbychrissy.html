Hair by Chrissy

Booking platform · Front end · Backend · Payments

Chrissy fits hair extensions by hand in a private London studio. Bookings came through Instagram DMs — a thread per client, no calendar, and no way to stop two people asking for the same Saturday.

So the site is not a brochure, it is the booking system. Pick a service, see genuine live availability, take a slot, pay a deposit. Behind it sits a real calendar, an admin view for Chrissy, deposit handling and automatic confirmation emails.

Built with no framework and no dependencies — a plain Node server, a JSON store and hand-written front end, so there is nothing to patch, nothing to renew and nothing to go stale.
