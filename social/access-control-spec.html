Administration — what a decision may and may not do
The only transitions that exist
Rules the database holds, not the interface
  • A decision requires a session that is signed in and second-factor verified. Password alone reads nothing.
  • Staff may write status, notes and decided-at. Never the applicant's own name, address or requested date.
  • Nobody can delete. A request is closed by moving its state, which leaves the trail intact.
  • Wrong details are declined and re-entered — so the record always shows what the person actually asked for.
  • Granting a place is not an update. That column carries no grant, so it goes through a function that re-counts capacity and refuses to overfill.
