LuxeScent UK — Luxury Car Diffusers

Website · design → deploy · brand & interaction build

A polished, shop-style storefront for a Bolton maker of designer-inspired car diffusers — a nine-strong fragrance collection with a product carousel and an interactive scent finder. Every “Shop” tap deep-links to the brand’s Etsy listings, so the site does the selling and Etsy takes the payment.
