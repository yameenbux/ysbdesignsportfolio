Taiyabah Masjid

PWA · Website · Signage · Serverless backend

A Bolton masjid needed prayer times in front of its community every day — on phones, on the wall, and on the web. Three separate problems, all being solved by hand.

I built one system: an installable app with live audio and push alerts, a public website, and two always-on display screens inside the building. A Python pipeline feeds all of them from a single timetable, so nothing is typed twice.

The community opens the app daily, the screens refresh themselves, and the new-build appeal runs in the foyer without anyone touching it.
